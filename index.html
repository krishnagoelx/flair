Built for the full shopping task.

Flair is designed to join shopping intelligence with purchase execution, order visibility, and a developer integration that respects the shopper’s control.

Does it only recommend?

No. The intended flow carries an authorized purchase through to a confirmed order.

Does my app lose the user?

The shopping experience and order result are designed to stay inside your app.

Can the AI spend freely?

No. Each order needs specific approval or a bounded rule the shopper can revoke.
